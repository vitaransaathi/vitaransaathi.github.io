How to install

  1. 1
    Tap Download next to your appEach person installs only the app for their work.
  2. 2
    Open the downloaded fileIf the phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or WhatsApp, once.
  3. 3
    Tap Install, open the app, log inLater updates come inside the app: a bar shows Update now. Your data stays.

Get started in five steps (for the owner)

  1. 1
    Get your company codeOpen Vitaran Owner and tap Start free trial, or get the code from the Vitaran team. Keep it safe.
  2. 2
    Log in as the ownerHome shows sales, collections and dues as they happen.
  3. 3
    Make logins for your teamVitaran Owner → Setup → Team: one login for each salesman, biller, godown and delivery person.
  4. 4
    Send each person their loginThe login message carries the link to their app. Send it on WhatsApp.
  5. 5
    Connect Tally on the office computerInstall the Tally Connector. In TallyPrime: F1 Help → Settings → Connectivity, act as Both, ODBC Yes, port 9000.
